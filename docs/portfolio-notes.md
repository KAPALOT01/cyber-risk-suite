# Portfolio Notes

## Positioning

This suite demonstrates a career direction at the intersection of cybersecurity consulting, GRC analysis, AI risk governance, and clear executive communication. It complements the security-operations detection lab on the GitHub profile.

## Demonstration integrity

- Treat scenario results as examples, not client outcomes.
- Do not claim reduced review time, risk exposure, or cost unless measured and documented.
- Do not describe the tool as certifying compliance or replacing an auditor, attorney, or system owner.
- Tailor frameworks, thresholds, terminology, and contract language to organizational scope and current criteria.

## Suggested demo flow

1. Show how AI deployment, data sensitivity, and external actions affect safeguards.
2. Explain the source of each GRC financial assumption before discussing ROI.
3. Show how a weak vendor evidence dimension drives a specific procurement action.
4. Close with the evidence a real consulting engagement would need before making a recommendation.
